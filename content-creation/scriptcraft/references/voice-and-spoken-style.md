# Natural, spoken creator voice

## Baseline

Sound like a technically curious person sharing a genuinely useful experiment with a friend. Friendly, direct, excited when the screen earns excitement. Clear without being patronising, precise without being an API reference. **Use the speaker's own register**, never impersonate a named creator.

### 10 spoken rules

1. Show something striking on camera before claiming it is striking.
2. Hook with what happened or what you will make, not "Hello, welcome back".
3. Use short clauses with varied rhythm: "Look at this. I only gave it one picture. Here's what happened." Avoid writing every line identically short.
4. Strong verbs and everyday nouns: open, upload, choose, change, run, see, copy.
5. First person only when the user really did the action or asks for a first-person persona.
6. Transition with causal purpose: "So I tried...", "Here is the part that matters", "Now watch what changes". Not automatic "Next up" at every line.
7. Keep names/terms stable; explain unfamiliar jargon at point of use, in as few words as possible.
8. Cut repeated adjectives and hollow praise: revolutionary, absolutely insane, super powerful, game-changing, seamless, unlock the power of.
9. No templated AI contrasts such as "not just X, but Y"; no wooden marketing prose, forced rhetorical questions, fake "you won't believe" moments.
10. The CTA should pay back attention: template, test file, prompt, code, checklist. Match user's exact wording if prescribed; do not add a CTA when unwanted.

## Natural rewrite in three passes

**Pass A – Trim:** remove redundant opening, double explanation, adjectives, unnecessary hedges, introductions to known products. Preserve truth qualifiers.

**Pass B – Speak:** shorten sentence where the breath naturally ends; replace abstract verbs with physical ones; make referents clear. Read aloud and fix awkward consonant groups, list-heavy clauses and similar repeated openings.

**Pass C – Show:** under every sentence ask "what is on the screen?" Either film it, mention a caption/evidence, or cut the sentence.

### Prosody and timing

As a rough *drafting* aid, start with approximately 2–2.6 spoken English words per second for quick social video, and less for demonstrations that need to breathe. Count beats/pauses and screen actions separately. Do **not** use word count as a guarantee; actual voice, language and editing can differ widely.

## Voice controls

- `energy`: restrained / warm / lively / surprised, subject to factual payoff
- `formality`: casual to professional
- `technical_depth`: novice / competent / expert
- `personal_experience`: verified yes/no
- `humor`: none / light / playful
- `localization`: UK English / Malaysian English / Chinese XHS etc.
- `avoid`: user-specific disliked constructions
- `positive_examples`: locally stored, rights-cleared examples only

Never claim a sample is "user-written" based solely on the fact that it appeared in a previous AI conversation. Track origin and approval separately.
