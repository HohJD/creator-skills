# Editorial evaluation rubric

A 100-point rubric is a project-quality tool, **not a scientific measure of virality**.

| Dimension | Max | Full marks look like |
|---|---:|---|
| Factual integrity | 20 | Claims have source/observed proof; no invented app steps |
| Natural voice | 15 | Easy to read aloud; human rhythm; source voice respected |
| Hook and payoff | 15 | Specific, filmed, credible first beat fulfilled later |
| Usable instruction | 15 | Viewer sees input/action/output; can reproduce key insight |
| Visual synchronisation | 10 | Narration maps directly to observable shots |
| Audience fit | 8 | Setup/jargon matches learner's actual starting point |
| Flow and pacing | 7 | No redundant setup; transitions have reasons |
| Compliance/CTA | 5 | Exact requested brief and disclosure preserved |
| Accessibility | 3 | Legible, understandable with captions/description |
| Next-step usefulness | 2 | One relevant viewer action/transfer |

## Publication blockers (any one overrides numeric score)

- Fabricated first-person use, output, benchmark, release date, savings or real-world footage.
- Product or command instruction known to fail or clearly unverified but spoken as fact.
- Misleading portrayal of AI video as official footage, gameplay, evidence or real person.
- Material sponsor disclosure absent, or required CTA altered without approval.
- Exposes secrets/private campaign terms or copied unauthorized creator transcripts.
- Puts unsafe/destructive action in instructions without relevant risk disclosure.

## Evaluate consciously, not mechanically

1. Mark blockers then score.
2. Suggest the **smallest rewrite** that repairs each issue.
3. Read voiceover aloud, check estimated length and pauses with real screen actions.
4. Explain why hook payoff is fulfilled, where proof happens, and what to re-record.
5. Never produce fabricated analytics or retrospective claims about retention.

Rubric categories are editorial synthesis informed by multimedia research; weights are project choices and should be evaluated empirically.
