# Visual prompt packs

Read the creative brief and inspect supplied reference assets when possible. Decide which attributes need to stay fixed (subject identity, product features, visual medium, palette, crop family) and which one changes per variant. Describe visible features plainly. Do not promise exact identity or product fidelity from a text prompt. For an actual product or screenshot, recommend using the supplied original as the source layer when fidelity matters.

Deliver a short art direction and independently pasteable prompts for each requested asset. Include the focal point, orientation, composition, lighting or mark-making and intended text placement. Express differences in what the viewer sees. A label like “more premium” needs a visible choice, such as a quieter background or tighter crop. For text-led assets, give a layout direction or editable overlay copy. Do not invent generator flags or unsupported platform settings. Keep any generated scene illustrative when the content requires documentary evidence.

For variations intended to inform a creative test, change one deliberate element at a time. If asked for prompts, provide prompts. If asked to generate assets, use the available authorised generation workflow and inspect the result before claiming fidelity.

## Copyable brief

> Make [number] standalone image prompts for [series]. Keep [palette, subject and aspect ratio] fixed. Vary only [distance/action/composition]. Leave [area] free for editable text.

## Worked example

**Fictional input.** A stationery maker wants two 4:5 illustrations of a folded paper boat. Navy ink, ivory paper and an empty top third are fixed. Vary only camera distance.

**Wide:** “4:5 portrait illustration, navy ink on ivory paper. One folded paper boat at rest, seen from the side. Wide framing with the whole boat small in the lower half. Keep the top third empty. No lettering.”

**Close:** “4:5 portrait illustration, navy ink on ivory paper. One folded paper boat at rest, seen from the side. Tight framing on the folded hull across the lower half. Keep the top third empty. No lettering.”
