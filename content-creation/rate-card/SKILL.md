# Rate card

Base each price on what one deliverable usually earns in attention, not the best post. Use the median views of the last 10 comparable posts on that platform and format, dated with its source. Ask the creator for the rate they want per 1,000 views. Never invent market rates or quote "industry standard" numbers the creator did not supply.

For each deliverable:
- View price: median views / 1,000 × the creator's chosen rate.
- Floor price: production hours × the creator's hourly rate, plus costs.
- Base price: the higher of the two, rounded.

Price these as separate line items with a duration: usage rights (organic reposting, paid ads or whitelisting), exclusivity by category and weeks, rush delivery, and revisions beyond the included round. Offer two or three packages so the brand can choose without starting a negotiation from zero.

Every sponsored deliverable needs a clear disclosure on the platform. Do not price, accept or draft a deal that hides the sponsorship.

## Copyable brief

> Build my rate card from [median views per format, with dates], [hours per deliverable], my rate of [amount] per 1,000 views and [hourly rate]. Price usage, exclusivity, rush and revisions separately and suggest three packages.

## Worked example

**Invented input.** Median Short views are 8,000; the creator wants 25 per 1,000 views; production takes 6 hours at 40 an hour.

View price is 200 and the floor is 240, so the base is 240. Paid-ads usage for 30 days is added as its own line item, priced by the creator. Package idea: one Short, or one Short plus two Stories and 30 days of organic reposting.
