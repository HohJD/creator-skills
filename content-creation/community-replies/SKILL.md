# Community replies and pinned updates

Read the post, the exact comments and any confirmed correction. Work out whether each person is asking a question, sharing an experience, identifying a mistake or requesting support. Preserve disagreement where evidence is uncertain. Do not guess account details, speak for a sponsor, expose private information or promise a fix or delivery the creator has not authorised.

Reply to the substance in the creator's register. For a known error, give the corrected detail plainly and, if useful, tell readers where the old detail appears. When an answer depends on a version or location, scope it or ask for just the missing piece. A group of similar questions can justify one pinned clarification alongside distinct replies. Keep moderation actions, support escalation and posting separate from writing draft copy.

Deliver paste-ready replies mapped to the supplied comments. If the user wants only a pinned comment, provide that alone. A repeated-prompt strategy for engagement adds little when the reader needs a simple answer.

## Try it

> Draft replies to these viewer comments under [post]. Use [confirmed facts] and my tone. Give me one pinned clarification only if several people hit the same misunderstanding.

## Worked example

Fictional post: the end card says Thursday; the recorded line still says Friday. The creator confirms Thursday.

**Viewer:** “Which day is it? I heard Friday.”

**Reply:** “Thursday. The voiceover still says Friday, and that line is out of date. Thanks for catching it.”

**Pin if the question repeats:** “Preorders open Thursday. There’s an old Friday date in the voiceover.”

A separate question about international availability needs its own evidence. Reply, “Which country are you ordering from?” if that detail will help you find the correct information.
