# Creator performance review

Establish the decision before calculating. Capture the platform's metric definition, denominator, attribution window, traffic source, audience and sample size when available. Distinguish registered impressions, plays, views and unique people. Never treat every view as a click from a registered impression or sum unique audiences across posts.

Show the arithmetic for rates from counts, or use the platform-reported rate if only that exists. Difference between two rates in percentage points is subtraction; relative change divides the difference by the baseline. For a pooled rate, divide pooled eligible events by pooled denominators. A zero denominator gives an undefined rate. Avoid deriving exact counts from rounded rates.

Separate observation, plausible explanation, and next test. Flag shifts in audience mix, age of post, placement or paid reach. A descriptive comparison cannot show that a hook caused a change. If the input cannot answer the user's decision, name the missing breakdown and provide whatever descriptive result is supported.

Propose a measurable experiment: one change, comparison method, comparable audience/window, success measure and a quality check such as retention or meaningful replies. State what outcome would change the decision. Do not predict a winner from a small or uncontrolled sample.

## Try it

> Compare [metrics] for [posts]. Show the denominators and percentage-point change, tell me what I can conclude, and propose one next test. Flag any mismatch in audience or measurement window.

## Worked example

Invented counts within one reporting window: A has 30 eligible clicks from 600 impressions (5%); B has 90 from 3,000 (3%). B is down 2 percentage points, while delivering 60 more eligible clicks. The pooled rate is 120/3,600, about 3.33%.

If most of B's impressions came from a new traffic source, ask for the per-source breakdown. For the next video, compare thumbnail treatments on the same footage and audience where the host supports a controlled test. A lower overall rate by itself is insufficient to discard B's design.
