# Creator benchmark

Confirm the target is comparable: same platform, similar format and a reachable size. When the target is more than 20 times larger, suggest a stepping-stone creator at 3 to 10 times first and keep the original as the long-term goal.

Collect only numbers the creator supplies or can read publicly, each with platform, date and source. Normalise before comparing: followers as a dated snapshot, views per post as the median of the last 10 comparable posts, posts per week over the same 8 weeks, engagement as (likes + comments + shares) / views with both counts shown. Never treat views as unique people or add followers across platforms as one audience.

Work out the plan:
- Gap: target followers minus current followers.
- Required monthly growth: (target / current)^(1 / months) − 1.
- Time at current pace: log(target / current) / log(1 + current monthly growth). Say "not measurable" without two dated follower snapshots at least 28 days apart. When the pace is zero or negative, say the target is not reachable at the current pace instead of calculating a time.
- With zero current followers, skip both formulas and set a first milestone, such as 100 followers, to measure from.

Then study the target's 10 most recent standout posts. Record topic, format, length, opening, packaging and posting day. Separate what you observed from what you infer. Paid promotion, an older platform era, a team or a viral outlier can explain a gap that you cannot copy.

Finish with two or three experiments. Each one changes one thing, runs four weeks, names its metric and its stop rule. Borrow patterns, not content. Do not copy their scripts, thumbnails, titles or catchphrases.

## Copyable brief

> Benchmark my [platform] channel ([followers on date], [median views], [posts per week]) against [target] ([their dated numbers]). Show the gap, the monthly growth I need for [months] and my time at current pace, then give me three one-variable experiments.

## Worked example

**Invented input.** A pottery channel has 1,200 followers and grows 6% a month. The target has 12,000. The creator wants 12 months.

Required growth is 10^(1/12) − 1 ≈ 21.2% a month. At 6% it takes log 10 / log 1.06 ≈ 39.5 months. The target posts three times a week to the creator's one, and its standout posts open on the finished piece. Experiment 1: post twice a week for four weeks with the same quality bar. Measure followers gained per week and stop if median views fall by more than a third.
