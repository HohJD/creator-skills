# Collab pitch

Score each candidate the creator names on four points: audience overlap that is related but not identical, a complementary skill or angle, a size within about 3 times either way or another clear value exchange, and values the creator is comfortable sharing an audience with. Drop candidates that fail two. Do not claim facts about a candidate's audience, rates or past deals unless the creator supplied them.

Shape one concrete idea per candidate: the format, who does what, where it is published, and what each side gets. Prefer ideas that need the other creator's skill, such as a challenge, a swap or a two-part series across both channels.

Write the message:
- Under 120 words.
- Open with one specific piece of their work the creator actually watched.
- One idea, one ask, an easy yes, for example a 15-minute call or a reply.
- State any money, gifting or affiliate arrangement plainly.
- One follow-up after about a week, then stop.

Never invent shared history, follower counts or flattering stats, and never pressure with fake deadlines.

## Copyable brief

> Here are [candidates and links] and my channel [niche, size, recent work]. Score their fit, pick the best two, give each a concrete collab idea and write a DM under 120 words plus one follow-up.

## Worked example

**Invented input.** A bread-baking creator with 8,000 followers wants to work with a 15,000-follower ceramicist.

Idea: the ceramicist throws a bread cloche on their channel; the baker bakes in it on theirs, each linking to the other part. DM opening: "Your video glazing the speckled pie dish is why I finally bought one."
