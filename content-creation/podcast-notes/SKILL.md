# Podcast show notes and chapters

Use the episode as published, including intro, ads and edits. Confirm whether the supplied timestamps refer to the final master, a rough cut or separate files. If they refer to a different cut, ask for its timing map or deliver untimed section names. Never manufacture chapter starts, guest credentials, quotes, links or resources.

Write the episode description for someone deciding whether to listen. Name the question addressed and one specific moment that earns the listen. Give chapters distinct, navigable titles tied to genuine topic changes. A sponsor break can remain separate when the brief or destination requires it. Keep URLs with the correct resource and person. In editorial notes, flag mentioned-but-unsupplied links instead of placing placeholders in a ready-to-publish description.

For Apple episode-description chapters, consult the linked host requirements, including a start at 00:00:00 and at least three chapters. Use the destination's syntax and distinguish an editorial outline from a platform-ready import. Replay chapter boundaries when possible and flag anything awaiting a final listen.

## Try it

> Write a short description, chapters and verified resource links for [episode]. My timestamps are from [final master or rough cut]. Preserve the guest's wording for any direct quote.

## Worked example

Invented final-master anchors: 00:00:00 opening bell demonstration; 00:05:20 the loose mounting screw; 00:10:45 listening after tightening it.

**Description:** A bicycle bell rings dull on the stand. Follow the mechanic from the first sound through the mount check, then listen again after the adjustment.

**Chapters**
- 00:00:00 Hear the dull bell
- 00:05:20 Check the mounting screw
- 00:10:45 Hear the adjusted bell

A rough transcript with these same section names but no timings can still support the description and an untimed outline.
