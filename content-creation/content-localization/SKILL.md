# Creator content localization

Establish the target language, writing system where relevant, audience and reading context. Use the creator's own target-language sample or glossary when available. Preserve their register. Ask about locale only when the choice changes the output materially. Never infer a regional price, offer, release, unit conversion or localized UI label from the language of the assignment.

Mark strings that must remain literal: names, promo codes, commands, interface labels shown on screen and required disclosure wording. Translate the surrounding explanation. Replace an idiom with the intended meaning when a literal rendering would sound strange. Keep uncertainty, comparisons and limitations just as strong as in the source.

Deliver in the requested format: local-language copy only, parallel columns or per-card text. For speech, give the line a spoken rhythm in the target language and leave space to perform the recorded action. For a bilingual card, make each language legible and check that the same action or fact appears in each. For a timestamped subtitle file, use the subtitle-editing workflow. Flag a term outside publishable copy when the product screenshot or approved translation is missing.

## Try it

> Adapt [copy] for [audience/language]. Keep the exact screen labels [labels] and preserve [qualifier]. Give me final copy and a short note on any term that still needs checking.

## Worked example

Fictional English source: “On my account, tap Save draft. You can come back to it later.” Audience reads Simplified Chinese; the recording uses English UI.

**Spoken Mandarin:** “我这个账号里，点一下‘Save draft’，就能先存成草稿，回头再接着改。”

Keep “我这个账号里” because the observation applies to one account. Keep “Save draft” because that's what the viewer needs to find in the recording.
