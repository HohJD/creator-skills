# Sponsor and UGC brief

Use the commissioning party's actual requirements. Sort them into required, suggested and awaiting confirmation. Capture platform, asset count, aspect or length, deadlines with timezone, review owner and any stated usage terms. Keep the desired viewer action separate from the sponsor's internal goal.

For each must-say claim, record the exact words, the strongest supplied evidence, and whether a creator can demonstrate it. Call out incompatible requirements and name who can resolve them. Preserve disclosures and where they are required. A demo on one account rarely supports a claim about all customers. Do not invent testimonials, personal use, usage rights, paid amplification, exclusivity, rates or rights periods.

Deliver a brief a creator could shoot from: audience, message, visual proof, mandatory wording, creative choices left open, deliverables and approval questions. When no product has been tried, a proposed shot remains a shot to capture. For final dialogue after agreement, hand the brief to a script-writing workflow.

## Copyable brief

> Make a one-page UGC brief from [campaign notes]. Separate mandatory copy from choices I can make. List the shots that establish each claim, delivery specs and unresolved approvals.

## Worked example

**Fictional input.** A sponsor asks for a 20-second Reel, the exact line “Made with Finch,” and a demo using the supplied zipped pouch and passport. The phrase “fits every passport” is also requested.

**Deliverable:** One 20-second Reel. **Show:** the supplied passport going into the pouch, followed by the zip closing. **Must say:** “Made with Finch.” **Creator choice:** top-down or hand-held, whichever shows the fit. **Resolve:** ask the sponsor for evidence or approval to narrow “fits every passport.” Confirm any disclosure wording and delivery date before the recording copy is locked.
