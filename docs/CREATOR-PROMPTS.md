# Creator prompt library

Replace the brackets with your material. In Codex, start with the skill name shown. Each entry links to a worked example and the sources behind it. All examples are fictional unless explicitly marked otherwise.

### Social captions

> $social-captions Write two captions for my [post] for [audience]. My rough line is [line]. Give me a behind-the-scenes version and a useful tip. Keep [wording], and use [CTA or no CTA].

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/social-captions/SKILL.md#worked-example)

### Subtitles and closed captions

> $subtitle-editor Use my timecoded transcript to make an English VTT. Keep the second click audible in the captions and preserve the verified cue boundaries. If only text is available, return an untimed draft.

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/subtitle-editor/SKILL.md#worked-example)

### Alt text and image descriptions

> $image-descriptions Write alt text for [image] in an article about [topic]. The adjacent caption already says [caption]. For a chart, give a short alt and a fuller text equivalent using my values.

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/image-descriptions/SKILL.md#worked-example)

### Video titles and thumbnails

> $title-thumbnail Give me a search-led and a browse-led title/thumbnail pair for [video]. The actual payoff is [result] at [moment]. Specify which real frame to use and keep any overlay short.

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/title-thumbnail/SKILL.md#worked-example)

### Shot lists and B-roll

> $shoot-plan Build a shoot-order list for [scene]. I have [time, gear and crew]. [Action] can happen only once. Give me essential coverage, sound and one optional pickup.

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/shoot-plan/SKILL.md#worked-example)

### Creator interview guide

> $interview-guide Prepare a [minutes]-minute interview with [guest] about [decision]. Give me a timed running order, one conditional follow-up per section and a closing fact check.

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/interview-guide/SKILL.md#worked-example)

### Creator newsletters

> $newsletter-editor Turn my [notes] into a short newsletter for [subscribers]. Give me a subject, a preheader that adds something new, and a body under [length]. The only link you may use is [URL or none].

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/newsletter-editor/SKILL.md#worked-example)

### Editorial planning

> $content-planner Plan [period] of content from [questions/assets]. I have [hours per week] and [hard deadline]. Show estimated time, dependencies, purpose and what to drop if the shoot slips.

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/content-planner/SKILL.md#worked-example)

### Content repurposing

> $content-repurpose Turn [source] into [two or three channels]. Give each a different purpose, include the copy, and show which passage supports it. Keep [qualifier] in every piece where it matters.

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/content-repurpose/SKILL.md#worked-example)

### Creator performance review

> $creator-analytics Compare [metrics] for [posts]. Show the denominators and percentage-point change, tell me what I can conclude, and propose one next test. Flag any mismatch in audience or measurement window.

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/creator-analytics/SKILL.md#worked-example)

### Podcast show notes and chapters

> $podcast-notes Write a short description, chapters and verified resource links for [episode]. My timestamps are from [final master or rough cut]. Preserve the guest's wording for any direct quote.

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/podcast-notes/SKILL.md#worked-example)

### Community replies and pinned updates

> $community-replies Draft replies to these viewer comments under [post]. Use [confirmed facts] and my tone. Give me one pinned clarification only if several people hit the same misunderstanding.

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/community-replies/SKILL.md#worked-example)

### Short clip selection

> $clip-selects Choose [number] clips from [transcript or recording] for [audience]. Give a paper edit with source locators, the opening and closing words, and anything I'd misrepresent by cutting too early.

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/clip-selects/SKILL.md#worked-example)

### Creator content localization

> $content-localization Adapt [copy] for [audience/language]. Keep the exact screen labels [labels] and preserve [qualifier]. Give me final copy and a short note on any term that still needs checking.

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/content-localization/SKILL.md#worked-example)

### Prepublication fact check

> $creator-fact-check Audit [draft plus materials]. Which claims can I publish as written? For each consequential issue, give me the evidence and the smallest accurate replacement. Mark what still needs a source.

[Worked example and sources](../content-creation/creator-fact-check/SKILL.md#worked-example)
