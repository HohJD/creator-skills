# Editorial trials · 21 November 2026

Independent Codex evaluators received fictional briefs, read the relevant local skill files and produced the requested content. The editor checked the returned copy against the brief and its source material. Each row describes one brief. These were read-only trials of the skill instructions. Automatic host discovery, audience preference, accessibility conformance and post performance remain unmeasured.

| Skill | Brief and observed result |
| --- | --- |
| Social captions | A creator supplied a patched tote photo and “I can find this bag by touch now.” The first caption kept the line. On review the evaluator tightened the second angle to avoid assuming the tote's age. |
| Subtitle editor | A correction at 100–1450 ms and a zipper sound at 1550–2410 ms became separate VTT cues. The word “not” and the silent gap survived. The published [example files](../examples/creator-captions/) use those intervals. |
| Image descriptions | A map pin was the only content of a directions link. The evaluator returned the accessible name “Directions to the workshop.” |
| Video titles and thumbnails | A desk lamp revealed the lettering on a real coin. The search title named the lighting task and the discovery title invited the reader to read the coin. The reviewer removed an unsupported assumption about the inscription's position. |
| Shot list | One phone and one match allowed one strike. The plan covered the unlit match, ignition and spent tip in a continuous take. |
| Interview guide | A six-minute conversation with a printmaker kept the cause of a registration error open and supplied a follow-up if the guest was unsure. |
| Content planner | A seven-day plan fit the 120-minute budget and Thursday-only capture slot. The fallback used the existing photo and withheld an unconfirmed stitch name. |
| Podcast notes | A rough-cut transcript referenced a different file. The evaluator gave usable show notes and untimed sections, and identified the final-master timings needed for publication. |
| Newsletter editor | The response described the wide cream mat and avoided a new purchase link or claim about which frame the artist preferred. Review of the bundled example caught a different unsupported preference, which was removed. |
| Content repurposing | Both an email opener and a post retained “this sleeve” and “one wash.” The supplied paragraph was credited outside the copy. The bundled example was revised to remove an invented sketchbook entry. |
| Creator analytics | 24/400 and 48/1,200 produced 6% and 4%, a fall of two percentage points. More attributed views and a changed traffic mix prevented a conclusion about the cover alone. |
| Community replies | Two viewers queried the closing date. The pinned correction stated Saturday at 6 pm London time and identified the old Sunday caption. |
| Clip selection | A phone success was immediately qualified by a tablet failure. The chosen passage retained both, located by paragraph and quoted boundaries because timestamps were unavailable. |
| Content localization | A Taiwan-facing line used Traditional Chinese, kept the English “Drafts” label visible in the screenshot, and preserved both device scope and uncertainty. |
| Creator fact check | An image of a Download button on one paid account supported only that observation. The free-for-everyone claim was marked unverified. |

A second newsletter and repurposing brief described two coats on a wall and its appearance at noon. Both outputs kept those as observations without attributing cause. That review also led to removing an unsupported photo arrangement and pencil-mark location from the bundled examples.

Use the [positive and boundary prompts](CREATOR-PROBES.md) for future routing trials. For output review, give the evaluator the brief before showing them this table. Repeat on the target host when model or platform behaviour matters. A timing claim needs actual media playback and naturalness in a target language deserves review by its intended speakers.
